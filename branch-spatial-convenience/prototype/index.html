BRANCH / SPATIAL CONVENIENCE
01 / service-aware wayfinding

Find the right outlet.

A spatial decision layer for businesses with multiple physical locations. Choose a service, compare capable outlets and route the customer toward the most convenient available option.
Fastest service

What do you need?

Information architecture

Network → Outlet → Capability → Action

01

Intent

Start with the customer's job to be done instead of the organisation's internal branch structure.

02

Capability

Represent each outlet as a service set: cash, FX, cards, business banking, accessibility and more.

03

Convenience

Combine distance, travel time, opening hours, service availability and optional queue data.

04

Route

Give the customer a recommended outlet, the reason, alternatives and the next action.

Applications

Not only banking.

05

Healthcare

Find a clinic with the required diagnostic or specialist capability.

06

Retail

Find stock, pickup, returns or specialist service at the most useful store.

07

Logistics

Find pickup, drop-off, international shipping or depot services.

08

Telecom

Route SIM replacement, device support and account services to capable outlets.

Prototype case study: Co-op Bank spatial branch exploration. Static demonstration data; live location, queue and routing APIs can replace the adapter layer.
